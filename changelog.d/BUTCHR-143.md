### Added
- **Every label butchr writes under its own namespaces (`agent:`, `pr:`, `butchr:`) must now declare who withdraws it, and the declaration is checked automatically (BUTCHR-143, part of the BUTCHR-133 epic).** `src/labels/registry.ts` is the one place all eleven labels — the five `agent:*` values, the four `pr:*` values, and the two verb-owned `butchr:shelved`/`butchr:orphan` — are declared together with a non-empty `withdrawnBy` string naming the mechanism that removes them. "Deliberately never withdrawn" is expressible too, but only as `withdrawnBy: null` paired with a required, non-empty `neverWithdrawnReason` — there is no optional field or boolean escape hatch.
- Two independent mechanisms enforce this, because they fail on different mistakes. A type-level door: `RegisteredLabel` is a closed union derived from `AgentLabel`/`PrState` (`src/labels/plan.ts`) plus the two verb-owned constants, and `LABEL_REGISTRY`'s `Record<RegisteredLabel, LabelRegistryEntry>` type fails to compile if a family grows without a matching entry, or if an entry omits `withdrawnBy`. A source-scanning door (`src/labels/label-scan.ts`, enforced live against `src/` in `test/unit/labels-registry.test.ts`): a real TypeScript-parser-based scan for whole-string label literals under the known namespaces, asserting each is registered — this is what actually catches a bare `const FOO = "butchr:foo"` declared outside the registry, the exact shape of bug that let `butchr:orphan` exist without a withdrawal path before BUTCHR-108.
- Both mechanisms' limits are written down rather than implied away: the source scanner sees declared literals only, and today finds zero of the nine `agent:*`/`pr:*` values (they're built by string concatenation, not literals) — coverage for those two families comes entirely from the type-level door instead. And a registry entry records that a withdrawal path *exists*, not that it's hole-free; `agent:stalled`'s entry notes the known gap in the startup sweep's JQL (BUTCHR-144, filed separately) as the live example.
- No runtime behavior changes: `src/labels/registry.ts` and `src/labels/label-scan.ts` are never imported by any label-writing code path (`src/labels/sync.ts`, `src/labels/sweep.ts`, `src/tools/relationship.ts`) — this is a declaration and a check, not a change to what gets written, when, or in what order.
