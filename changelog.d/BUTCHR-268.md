### Fixed
- **`agent.start` was firing on a pane `workspace.create` had just returned, before herdr considered it an "available shell" (BUTCHR-268).** The spawn path (`HerdrHerd.spawn()`, `src/agents/herd.ts`) called `workspace.create` then `agent.start` on the returned root pane back-to-back, with no gap. Measured directly against a live herdr via `HerdrClient` (`scripts/repro-pane-busy.ts`, 8 trials/gap, repeated — a single-sample sweep passed at gap=0 and is exactly the trap this ticket's own history warns about): at gap=0ms `agent.start` rejected with `agent_pane_busy` on **4/8** trials on this branch's own commit (the ticket that filed this measured 6/8 on a separate run); at gap≥50ms it was 0/8 every time. Re-measured against this fleet's live journal for 2026-09-10 00:00–11:34 (servyboi, `butchr.service`, build 467c36e8, unmodified — this is the fix's own "before"): 42 `agent_pane_busy` rejections out of 74 logged spawn/respawn attempts (57%), spanning 26 distinct tickets, producing 3 `[reconcile]` complaints and 1 `[crashloop]` complaint — the crashloop one (BUTCHR-279) confirmed self-healed within ~2 minutes of posting, i.e. a healthy startup that still cost two "go investigate" comments. Concurrency was measured and ruled out as the driver (flat ~60% across a 3x change in simultaneous create+start pairs) — this fix does not touch the reconcile loop's spawn fan-out.
- **Fix: wait before every `agent.start` attempt, and retry ONLY `agent_pane_busy`, bounded.** `HerdrHerd` now waits `PANE_READY_WAIT_MS` (200ms) before each attempt — including the first — and retries up to `PANE_BUSY_MAX_RETRIES` (4) additional times when, and only when, the rejection carries `HerdrError`'s `agent_pane_busy` code; any other `agent.start` rejection (including a herdr transport outage, where `agent.list`/`pane.read` fail with `socket error` and every tracked pane reads `pane_not_found`) still reaches `spawn()`'s own catch on the first attempt unchanged, so it can't become a hot loop, and the pane it just created still gets closed (BUTCHR-111's leak-safety guarantee — covered by a dedicated test). Worst case this adds `PANE_READY_WAIT_MS * (1 + PANE_BUSY_MAX_RETRIES)` = 1000ms to a failing spawn, negligible next to `KICKOFF_VERIFY_MS`'s existing multi-second wait, against a failure that used to cost a whole poll cycle. Re-measured with the fix's own logic (`scripts/repro-pane-busy.ts fixed`, same live herdr, gap=0, the worst case the table above found): **0/16** `agent_pane_busy` rejections reached the caller.
- **Harness hygiene.** `scripts/repro-pane-busy.ts` (new, manual/operator-only — not wired into `bun run check`, matching `verify-workspace-ground-truth.ts`/`reap-dry-run.ts`) sweeps its own leftover workspaces by label (`repro-268`/`err-check`/`repro` prefixes) at the START of every run, not only in a `finally` — a mid-run kill (e.g. a herdr-server restart) never reaches a `finally`, and this fleet's journal shows that restart-and-respawn pattern happening. Every sweep candidate is verified against a fresh `agent.list()` before being closed, and the ownership predicate is scoped to this harness's own label prefixes only — it never touches BUTCHR-111's reaper or its cwd-based ownership check. This run's own census on this herd found zero residue before and after.

### Limitations, stated deliberately rather than left implied
- **Not a guarantee, a bounded improvement.** The rate table's own 1/8 outlier at gap=1200ms means no fixed wait makes this impossible, only unlikely; a spawn can still exhaust the retry budget and fail. That failure still closes its pane and surfaces exactly as before — this fix narrows the window, it does not remove it.
- **No true fleet-level "after" in this fragment.** The "after" measurement above replays the fix's own retry logic against live herdr directly (`scripts/repro-pane-busy.ts fixed`), not the deployed daemon — merged is not deployed, and the running build's actual fleet-wide busy/complaint rate after this ships should be re-measured against the journal once it is.
- **`[reconcile]`/`[crashloop]` complaint thresholds and text are unchanged** — out of scope for this ticket by design; if complaints still fire post-deploy on healthy startups, that is a detector-calibration question, not this fix's.
