### Added
- **Resume a jira-work worker's prior Claude session on respawn** (epic FACTORY-843, story FACTORY-850, closing out siblings FACTORY-848/FACTORY-849): when the daemon respawns a jira-work Claude worker whose rule has `resumeOnRespawn` enabled (the default), whose previous stop classifies as UNINTENDED (`classifyStop`, FACTORY-849), and whose persisted Claude session id and transcript both still exist and estimate under the rule's `resumeContextCutoff`, the new agent now launches with `--resume <id>` and continues the prior conversation on a brand-new pane, instead of always starting fresh. Every other outcome (flag off, an intentional stop, no persisted session id, a missing transcript, or an over-cutoff transcript) logs one greppable `[resume] <issue> respawn-fresh — <reason>` line and falls back to today's ordinary fresh spawn — unchanged behaviour for every rule that doesn't opt in, and for every workspace with nothing to resume. The transcript-size check is a cheap `stat()`-based byte-count ESTIMATE (never parses the file), documented as such.
- A resumed respawn posts a new, third ticket notice (`[butchr:respawn-resume]`, distinct from the existing `[butchr:respawn]`/`[butchr:resume]` markers): it tells the agent the conversation is its own prior one (not fresh), but — unlike an in-place `resumeInPlace()` resume, where the process never actually stopped — its process really was gone for a time, so it must still re-read its ticket before acting, since the brief or ticket may have changed while it was down.
- Closes a gap FACTORY-852 found in FACTORY-849's own `clearStopCause`: a worker's durable stop-cause marker (written by `stand_down`, `submit_to_boss`, `finish_without_a_boss`, `finish_worker`, or `shelve_worker`) is now cleared at the exact point a respawn/resume for that workspace is decided — not only by `startWorker`/`adoptWorker("start")` — so one intentional stop never silently suppresses resume on a LATER, genuinely unintended crash with no intervening `start_worker`/`adopt_worker` call in between.
