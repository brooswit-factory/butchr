### Fixed
- **Session-limit recognition now fires on the real `⎿`-prefixed banner, without closing a pane that merely displays the phrase (BUTCHR-259/BUTCHR-241).** `detectSessionLimitRefusal`'s anchor never matched a genuine banner in production: every real refusal renders as a `⎿`-prefixed tool-result line (`20 20 e2 8e bf 20 c2 a0` — measured 97/97 in a preserved 52-file real-capture corpus), and `.trim()` strips the leading whitespace/NBSP but not `⎿` itself, so the anchored `/^You(?:'|')ve hit.../` could never match it at any `TAIL_LINES` window size. Recovery was never scheduled, not merely late — 4h43m across two measured outages.
- Fixing the anchor alone is also insufficient: the same corpus measurement found genuine banners sitting at content-line depth 5–25 from the end of the pane (composer chrome, a completion-status line, and the banner's own wrapped continuation always intervene), so the existing `TAIL_LINES = 4` window recognises none of them either — both gates were shut simultaneously.
- The fix does not widen `TAIL_LINES` as the primary mechanism (a wide fixed or vocabulary-bounded window was measured to still miss real banners behind arbitrary async notification-box chrome, and to still risk false-closing a healthy pane at the tail end of the distribution). Instead, a `⎿`-prefixed candidate is recognised only when it is (a) at exactly margin depth 2 (the pane's own top-level tool result — not nested inside some other, enclosing tool result, which renders at depth ≥5) and (b) immediately paired with its own real wrap continuation (`/upgrade` or `/usage-credits…`, measured 100% present on every genuine banner) with nothing further embedded after it. `TAIL_LINES` is kept only for a legacy bare-line (no `⎿` prefix) path, unchanged from before.
- Every outcome is now legible in the log and in a new `classifySessionLimitText` API: `recognised-and-scheduled`, `recognised-but-suppressed-with-reason` (a suppression always states which structural signal fired), or `not-recognised` — replacing a bare `null` that made "detection is broken" and "nothing to detect" look identical.
- A named, bounded residual risk is documented rather than silently left open: a tool result whose own output is byte-identical to a genuine banner+continuation pair, at margin depth 2, is currently indistinguishable from a live refusal from pane text alone (verified: no further structural signal exists to separate them in `pane.read`'s flattened, ANSI-stripped text). Mitigated by the pre-existing `agent_status` idle/done poller gate and by the narrow, largely epic-internal reproducibility of the exact bytes.
