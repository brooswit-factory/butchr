### Fixed
- **Both brief-coverage guards (`test/unit/merge-check-guard.test.ts` and `test/unit/assertion-check-guard.test.ts`) now tie their hand-written positive-assertion lists to `knownBriefTypes()`, so a new `briefFor()` entry reddens the suite until it is explicitly asserted or excluded (BUTCHR-219/BUTCHR-225).** Before this: adding a new issue type to `src/agents/workspace.ts`'s `BRIEF_BY_TYPE` table shipped a brand-new agent-facing brief that neither guard's positive lists (`MERGE_INSTRUCTING_BRIEFS`/`REVIEW_LINE_INSTRUCTING_BRIEFS` in one file, the hand-written `["Story","Epic"]` loop in the other) nor their `EXCLUSIONS` arrays ever mentioned, and the suite stayed green — verified live: a neutral `briefs/probe.md` added to the table left `bun test test/unit test/load` at the same 1735 pass/0 fail as baseline.
- Each file now derives, from `knownBriefTypes()`, every member the file must account for in **exactly one** of: the union of its own positive-assertion list(s), or a new type-keyed `TYPE_EXCLUSIONS` array carrying a written, non-empty reason — never both, never neither. A pure `findUnaccountedBriefTypes()` helper (self-contained per file, not a shared import, matching this repo's `label-scan.ts`/`header-scan.ts`/`workspace-scan.ts` precedent) does the accounting; `formatUnaccountedBriefTypeError()` names the unaccounted type(s) and both ways to fix it.
- `merge-check-guard.test.ts`'s `TYPE_EXCLUSIONS` is empty today — the union of its two existing positive lists already covers every derived member (`task`+`story` from `MERGE_INSTRUCTING_BRIEFS`, `project`+`epic`+`story` from `REVIEW_LINE_INSTRUCTING_BRIEFS`) — left as an explicit, checked empty list, same precedent `family-scan.test.ts`'s `KNOWN_FAMILY_COLLISION_EXCLUSIONS` sets.
- `assertion-check-guard.test.ts`'s previous `EXCLUSIONS` entry lumped two real family members (`briefs/task.md`, `briefs/project.md`) together with `briefs/default.md` (not a family member — the untyped fallback, deliberately outside `knownBriefTypes()`) under one reason. That entry is split: `task` and `project` move to the new type-keyed `TYPE_EXCLUSIONS` with their own restated reasons, each checked against the derived family; `default.md`'s note stays as path-keyed prose in the old `EXCLUSIONS` list, since an entry naming it in `TYPE_EXCLUSIONS` would itself be flagged stale (it is never a member of `knownBriefTypes()`).
- A stale exclusion (naming a type no longer in `knownBriefTypes()`) now fails loudly in both files, and an exclusion with an empty/whitespace reason fails too — both mutation-verified, along with the headline mutation (an injected `probe` type reddens both files' coverage tests, naming `probe`), removing a type from a positive list (reddens naming exactly that type), and marking a type both asserted and excluded (reddens, proving "exactly one of").
- `briefs/CLAUDE.md` and `briefs/default.md` remain explicitly out of scope for this accounting (documented in both files) — neither is a `knownBriefTypes()` member.
- `test/unit/workspace.test.ts` and `test/unit/family-scan.test.ts` were confirmed out of scope: the former hand-lists issue types in many loops but makes no brief-coverage claim and has no exclusion list; the latter's `EXCLUSIONS`-shaped list covers a different family (label collisions, not briefs). Enumeration method: `grep -rln 'EXCLUSION' test/` (three hits: the two files this fix touches, plus `family-scan.test.ts`) cross-checked against `grep -rn 'knownBriefTypes\|briefFor' --include=*.ts .` and a manual read of each hit for an actual coverage claim.
