### Added
- **`finish_without_a_boss` — the dedicated route to Done for the one caller shape none of the other relationship verbs reach: a ticket with no boss at all (today, in practice, an Epic) (BUTCHR-39, per the settled glossary — https://wroosbit.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/BUTCHR/pages/12484678).** `submit_to_boss` only ever targets In Review, and `finish_worker` only ever fires from a boss reviewing one of its OWN workers; neither reaches a bossless ticket, so its only prior route to Done was `jira_transition`, sitting on the alias-removal clock with no successor. Takes NO ARGUMENTS — the only ticket it can ever act on is the caller's own, so there is nothing to get wrong — and moves that ticket to Done, but ONLY when the caller has no boss.
- **The refusal is the entire point of the verb, not a guard on top of it.** A caller that HAS a boss is refused, with a message that teaches: it names the boss, says to call `submit_to_boss` instead and let that boss call `finish_worker`, and says why — every Done in this system requires a second identity to have looked at the work first, and a caller with a boss already has that second identity waiting. This is the same move `prioritize_worker` makes refusing the caller's own key.
- **Designed to narrow to nothing on its own, not to be removed.** A planned tier above epics does not exist yet; if it did, every top-level ticket would have a boss and this verb would simply stop having callers, with no removal ever needed. Whether a top-level ticket should be able to close itself at all — with no second identity ever looking — is an open question this verb does not settle; that is the human's call, for if and when that tier gets built.
- `jira_transition` is otherwise unchanged this release and remains an alias for every other transition it can still make; its description now names `finish_without_a_boss` alongside the four other successors it already pointed to.
- The glossary gains a general naming rule for future verbs: a verb with a required argument should name what the agent owes; a verb with no arguments should name its precondition, since the precondition is its entire content. And a known narrowing in `file_where_it_belongs`'s (BUTCHR-37) Case B is now recorded in its doc comment: the notice can land on an agent-owned ticket, not a human-watched one, when the filer's own Implements chain is itself bossless — a known limitation, not fixed here, alongside a note that Case B's missing link is deliberate and will not feel deliberate to a future reader tempted to "fix" it.
