### Added
- **Lizard mode can now play a sound on the daemon's own host every time an auto-answer approves a permission prompt.** Opt-in, OFF by default (`BUTCHR_LIZARD_APPROVAL_SOUND`, unset/empty = no sound, today's behaviour exactly). With no override, the sound is `@brooswit/drovr`'s own bundled `assets/sounds/lizard-button.mp3`, resolved straight out of the installed package; `BUTCHR_LIZARD_APPROVAL_SOUND_PATH` optionally overrides it with a local file path (`~` expanded). Played once per approval via whichever local player is found first (`gst-play-1.0`, `afplay`, `mpv`, `ffplay`, `paplay`, `pw-play`, `aplay` — wav only), remembering the first one that actually works and falling back through the rest if a player fails at runtime; coalesces a burst of approvals into at most one sound per ~1.5s; logs one journal line per actual play (player, file, exit status). Never blocks, delays, or fails an approval: an unresolvable source or no usable player each log at most one warning and degrade to permanent silence rather than retrying or affecting the approval outcome (FACTORY-100/FACTORY-103). See `docs/permission-answer-loop.md`'s "Approval sound" section.
