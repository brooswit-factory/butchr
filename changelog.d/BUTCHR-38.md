### Changed
- **All five brief templates (`briefs/CLAUDE.md`, `default.md`, `epic.md`, `story.md`, `task.md`) now teach BUTCHR-35's relationship verbs instead of the generic Jira/Confluence tools they used to instruct agents to call by hand.** Staffing, review, and self-reporting instructions now name `new_worker`/`start_worker`/`shelve_worker`/`adopt_worker`/`finish_worker`/`prioritize_worker`/`tell_worker` (boss → worker) and `report_to_boss`/`ask_boss`/`submit_to_boss` (worker → boss) in place of `jira_create_issue`, `jira_transition`, `jira_assign`, and `jira_set_priority`. `jira_get_issue`, `jira_search`, `jira_add_comment`, `confluence_search_pages`, and `confluence_list_spaces` are taught explicitly as permanent, with `jira_add_comment` named as the deliberate sideways peer-to-peer channel the hierarchy-shaped verbs don't cover. `new_worker`'s required disposition and automatic role/link inference also removed two standing instructions that are now structural rather than manual: an epic/story no longer has to hand-carry an "assignment policy" (accountIds) down to its children's tickets, and a story no longer links itself to its epic — both are now done by the boss's own `new_worker`/`adopt_worker` call.
- **The optional "captain's log" convention is retired from every brief and replaced by "keep your doc current."** Removed: the dated-entry section, the `Log — <KEY> — <timestamp>` title format, the "write at least one entry" ritual, and the pointer to the old convention page. In its place, every brief now explains that the ticket's own Confluence doc (created with the ticket, already linked and nested) holds what is true now while ticket comments remain the event stream, states the human's reason for the change (a current doc means nobody has to fire an ancient agent back up to ask what happened), and teaches `set_doc`'s full-body-replace semantic (read with `get_doc()`, edit, write the whole thing) and its refusal to write real content over a still-provisional title. The epic brief's closing-summary instruction changed accordingly: the closing summary is now the final state of the epic's own doc via `set_doc`, not another ticket comment.
- **Epic's and story's review checklists now require checking the worker's doc against what actually shipped and rejecting on staleness**, alongside the existing formal-GitHub-review and `[review]` protocol (now sent via `tell_worker`).
- **Every brief points at the ASSIST Confluence space** as a durable reference for how the factory works, how to verify a claim in it, and how it fails — without summarizing the space's contents into the briefs themselves.
- **Two silent traps are now named by name** in the briefs that ask an agent to verify something: `jira_search` omitting issue links and priority (verify a link with `jira_get_issue` instead), and an approval sha going stale between review and merge (compare the reviewed `headRefOid` against the branch's current head before merging, never trust `reviewDecision` alone). Also added: naming `tell_worker` explicitly for the `ANSWER <n> <fingerprint>` reply that unfreezes a blocked child, with the specific caveat that `tell_worker` prepends the caller's identity tag to the first line of its text — so an `ANSWER` line sent as the entire message is silently unrecognized by the daemon's parser (the tagged line no longer starts with `ANSWER `) and the blocked worker never wakes up; the fix is to lead with a line of prose so the `ANSWER` line lands on its own.
- **Every brief now states its scope-fence obligation** (out-of-scope work gets filed outside the current epic, and naming where it belongs is half the job) with a clearly greppable placeholder for the not-yet-merged orphan-filing verb (`file_where_it_belongs`), rather than teaching the `jira_create_issue` orphan escape it replaces.
- BUTCHR-13's additive parked-child prose in `epic.md`/`story.md` is preserved and integrated with `shelve_worker`/the `butchr:shelved` label rather than reverted.
- `test/unit/workspace.test.ts`'s existing brief-content assertions are updated (not deleted) to match the new instructions, plus new guards for the replace-not-append semantic, the doc-staleness rejection criterion, the relationship-verb vocabulary per tier, and — the cheapest guard against a partial revert — that the old captain's-log title format and convention-page link no longer appear in any brief.
