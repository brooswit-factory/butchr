### Added
- **A repeatable verifier and a full measurement, settling whether a submitted GitHub PR review's recorded commit (`commit_id`/`reviews[].commit.oid`) is immutable — it is not, and the failure mode is narrower and stranger than the epic's founding observations suggested (BUTCHR-138).** `scripts/verify-review-commit-immutability.ts` (`snapshot <owner/repo> <pr-number> [out-file]` / `diff <a.json> <b.json>`) reads all four surfaces the review protocol depends on (REST list, REST by id, GraphQL, `reviewDecision`+`headRefOid`) and makes T0/T1 comparisons a one-command operation instead of a hand-reconstructed incantation — same manual/operator-check precedent as `scripts/verify-workspace-ground-truth.ts` and `scripts/verify-spawn-effort.ts`, not wired into `bun run check`, because it needs a live PR and a cross-account review a CI sandbox cannot promise.
- **`docs/review-commit-immutability.md`, with raw JSON snapshots under `docs/review-commit-immutability/snapshots/`, records the full measurement**: a controlled two-arm contrast (base-merge moves the record; a plain commit does not) plus a three-review chain discriminator, two "evil merge" trials, and independent verification of a seventh case (PR #151) that killed a two-condition mechanism the ticket had converged on mid-measurement. Six candidate mechanisms were proposed over the course of the work and all six were killed by direct evidence; the property that survives every one of nine measured cases is stated instead of a mechanism: the recorded commit equals the current head only when the branch's own contribution is unchanged since the review. No structured API surface is immutable (REST and GraphQL move together), closing the "read a different surface" remedy the epic was holding open; the review body and a machine-generated `[review] ... @ <sha>` Jira line both are, though the latter's hand-transcription risk was caught live during this measurement (a 39-character, non-existent sha typo, corrected before use) and is recorded as a reason that remedy needs validation, not just append-only storage.
