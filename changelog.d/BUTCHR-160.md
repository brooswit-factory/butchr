### Fixed
- **`createStalledCheck`'s comments-fetch failure no longer resolves to a confident `agent:stalled` (BUTCHR-160/BUTCHR-121).** `check` caught a failed comments fetch, returned `[]`, and fed that straight into `!rows.some(...)` — so "the fetch failed" and "the agent has never spoken" produced the identical `true`, meaning a transient Jira read failure could label a healthy, silent-but-fine ticket `agent:stalled`. `check` now returns `Promise<boolean | null>`, with `null` meaning "could not verify" — a third outcome distinct from both "confirmed not stalled" and "confirmed stalled". This brings `stalled.ts` into line with the house convention already established by `src/agents/parked.ts` and `src/agents/escalation-loop.ts`, both of which treat a failed comments read as something other than a confident empty result.
- `src/labels/sync.ts`'s `syncLabels` (`stalled.check`'s single caller) now treats `null` as "leave the `agent:*` label dimension exactly as it is applied this poll" — neither adding nor removing `agent:stalled` — and logs it distinctly (`WARNING: [labels] <key> stalled check could not verify ...`), matching the `WARNING:`-prefixed convention `parked.ts` already uses. On a `null` poll the candidate fed to the flap-stabilizer is the currently-applied label itself, not the raw observed status — falling through to the observed status would have made a ticket already carrying `agent:stalled` lose that label after two consecutive could-not-verify polls, through the exact same 2-poll confirmation the stabilizer uses for a real transition. `StalledTracker.observe` still runs on every poll (including one whose comments fetch fails), so `firstObservedAt`/`streakBroken` are unaffected and the next successful poll re-examines the same streak — this costs a delay, not the signal, for both the tracker's internal bookkeeping and the label itself.
- Deliberately unchanged in this PR: no Jira comment and no new health-surface component for a stalled-check verification failure — a `WARNING:`-prefixed `deps.log` line, matching the two sibling detectors, is judged the right scope; a cross-detector "a detector could not verify this poll" signal is a separate concern spanning `parked.ts`/`escalation-loop.ts` too, not specific to this ticket.
- This fix removes a false assertion; it does not yet make the detector's coverage visible to a reader of the board — a reader seeing no `agent:stalled` still can't distinguish "confirmed not stalled" from "the detector couldn't check." That gap is tracked separately as BUTCHR-164.
