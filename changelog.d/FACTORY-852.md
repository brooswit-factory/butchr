### Added
- **Durable per-workspace stop-cause record and an exported intentional-vs-unintended classifier (FACTORY-849/FACTORY-852).** `src/agents/stop-cause.ts` adds `workspaceStopCause`/`persistIntentionalStop`/`clearStopCause` — a small `.butchr-stop-cause.json` file per workspace, modelled on `workspaceSessionId`'s own ENOENT-fail-safe discipline, deliberately NOT the in-memory stand-down registry (which a daemon restart resets and is scoped to a different question). `recordIntentionalStop`/`clearIntentionalStop` fan the write/clear out across every rule-matched workspace for a resource. The `stand_down`, `finish_worker`, `shelve_worker`, `submit_to_boss` and `finish_without_a_boss` call sites now record an intentional stop; `startWorker`/`adoptWorker(..., "start")` clear a stale marker on reactivation. `classifyStop` is a pure, total function over (recorded marker, ticket status, ticket labels) returning `"intentional"` or `"unintended"`, with an undeterminable cause resolving to `unintended` per FACTORY-843. Classifier only — not wired into the spawn/respawn path (FACTORY-850's job).
