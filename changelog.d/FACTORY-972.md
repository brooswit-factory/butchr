### Added
- An `agent:*` label transition to `agent:stalled` now wakes the stalled ticket's boss (the other end of its own outward Implements link), mirroring FACTORY-949's `agent:blocked` wake: debounced per ticket (`BUTCHR_STALLED_WAKE_DEBOUNCE_MINUTES`, default 30 minutes) and deduped against stall-remediation.ts's own `[butchr:stall]` marker for the same episode. Never wakes the stalled ticket's own watcher or a sibling's watcher; the project-manager side of the wake is implemented but not yet wired into the live daemon loop (same pre-existing gap as the `agent:blocked` wake; tracked in FACTORY-976). Every other `agent:*`/`pr:*` transition stays exactly as silent as before.
- NEW: a hard per-recipient hourly rate cap on delivered stalled wakes (`BUTCHR_STALLED_WAKE_MAX_PER_HOUR`, default 6) — a boss that would otherwise receive more than this many stalled wakes in a rolling hour has the excess counted and logged (`/health`'s `stalledWakesCapped`) rather than delivered. Delivered wakes are logged (`[wake] stalled key=<k> recipients=boss`) and counted (`/health`'s `stalledWakes`).
