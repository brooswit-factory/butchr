### Fixed
- **A herdr that accepts a `subscribe()` connection and never acks no longer leaks a socket on every retry (FACTORY-751/FACTORY-775).** `withHerdrSubscribeDeadline`'s deadline-vs-subscribe race (FACTORY-722) correctly closed a LATE-ARRIVING ack after its own deadline, but a herdr that never acked at all left that promise unsettled forever — `sub.close()` was never reached, and `permission-answer-watch.ts`'s retry loop (`scheduleReconnect`) opened another raw connection every `(herdrCallTimeoutMs + resubscribeDelayMs)`, roughly one leaked socket per cycle, unbounded over time. `@brooswit/herdr-sdk`'s `subscribe()` exposes no way to abort an in-flight attempt (confirmed against its own source), so the fix is the daemon-side guard the finding named: `subscribeAgentStatus` (src/daemon/index.ts, now backed by `createOutstandingGuard` in the new `src/daemon/herdr-subscribe-deadline.ts`) never starts a second raw subscribe while a prior one has neither settled nor been closed. At most ONE raw connection is ever outstanding past this fix — not zero: a herdr that never acks at all still holds that one connection open forever, which nothing here (or the SDK) can reclaim.
- **`BUTCHR_HERDR_TIMEOUT_MS` now has an upper bound (300000ms / 5 minutes).** Previously `loadConfig` only checked `Number.isFinite(x) && x > 0`, so a value above 2147483647ms made bun's `setTimeout` silently wrap and fire in ~1ms instead of waiting — the exact opposite of what a larger timeout was meant to do. A value above the new ceiling now throws at startup (same `throw new Error(...)` shape every sibling knob already uses) instead of being silently accepted. The knob stays env-only/read-only in `SETTINGS_DEFINITIONS` (src/web/settings-api.ts) — deliberately NOT added to `SETTINGS_ALLOWLIST` (src/settings/settings-file.ts): that allowlist's `positiveMsWithFloor` kind is shared with the unrelated `BUTCHR_POLL_STALE_MS`, and giving it a per-key ceiling would be new write-path surface entangling two independently-tuned knobs; this fragment's change is a tighter load-time validation of an already-existing env var, not new surface.
