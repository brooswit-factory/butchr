### Changed
- **`report_to_boss`/`ask_boss`'s tool descriptions, `briefs/epic.md`, `briefs/project.md`, and the escalation loop's blocked-dialog follow-up comment now say plainly that an Epic reaches its project boss only while the epic is In Review.** An epic's `report_to_boss`/`ask_boss` comment always lands on the epic's own ticket; a project reads an epic's ticket comments only while that epic is In Review (the daemon's own `project IN (...) AND issuetype = Epic AND status = "In Review"` scan), so while an epic is In Progress, what it posts sits unread — no agent is woken by it. The same is NOT true for a Story or a Task: their boss watches their ticket regardless of status, and neither description was weakened.
- `briefs/epic.md` now says explicitly, in its blocked-dialog escalation section, that `submit_to_boss` is not a doorbell — moving to In Review to be heard misuses the review hop — and, for an In Progress epic with no safe option, tells it to call `report_to_boss` anyway the moment it recognizes it's blocked: only a person reading the ticket directly will ever see it, and no agent will answer.
- `briefs/project.md` now names its inbound surfaces precisely — a root-doc BODY/version edit, a root-doc COMMENT, and the ticket comments of epics it currently has In Review — states that an In Progress epic cannot reach it through any of them, and corrects the standing misconception that an epic's `report_to_boss` lands on the project's root doc — it lands on the epic's own ticket.
- The escalation loop's blocked-dialog follow-up comment (posted on any blocked Epic, Story, or Task) no longer unconditionally promises it "escalates to whoever watches you" — a promise that was false for an In Progress epic. It now states the Story/Task-vs-Epic distinction directly, in one tier-neutral sentence (issue type is not cheaply available at that call site).

No verb, wake edge, predicate, or route changed — words only.
