### Added
- **Per-rule admission rate limiting: `maxNewPerTick` / `minSecondsBetweenAdmissions` (FACTORY-907, epic FACTORY-906).** Two new optional `Rule` fields let an operator gentle one rule's own lane of admissions, independent of the fleet-wide `BUTCHR_MAX_AGENTS` cap: `maxNewPerTick` bounds how many NEW agents that rule may have admitted on a single poll, and `minSecondsBetweenAdmissions` bounds how often (wall-clock) that rule may admit at all — both set means both must allow. Absent (either or both) is today's behaviour exactly, byte-for-byte: no log change, no ordering change. A candidate a rule's own limit defers is never dropped — it shares the exact wait ledger (`orderByWait`) a cap-withheld candidate already uses, so it is retried next poll in the same fair order and never marked stalled/blocked/withheld-by-cap. A deferral logs a new, distinct `[admission2] rate-limit rule=<id> admitted=<a> deferred=<d> maxNewPerTick=<n> minSeconds=<m> next-in=<s>s` line, throttled to at most once per rule per 60 seconds. Both fields are validated in `rules.json` and surfaced read-only in `GET /api/rules`; they are not yet on the web write surface. See `docs/execution-modes.md`'s own new section for the full contract and an example, and the measured load5 story this closes.
